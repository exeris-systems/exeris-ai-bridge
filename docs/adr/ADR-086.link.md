# ADR-086 — Bound the AI Execution Layer to Observation Before Routing (link stub)

**Authoritative copy:** [`exeris-docs/adr/ADR-086-bound-the-ai-execution-layer-to-observation-before-routing.md`](https://github.com/exeris-systems/exeris-docs/blob/main/adr/ADR-086-bound-the-ai-execution-layer-to-observation-before-routing.md)

ADR-086 is a **cross-repo** decision owned by `exeris-docs`. It places the AI execution layer in `exeris-ai-execution`, holds it to observation before routing, and fixes the direction of every seam into it — including the one from this repository.

## Why this stub exists

§A.3 binds this repository: **`exeris-ai-bridge` is a context adapter to the execution layer, under ADR-025 — never its host.** The bridge's mission runs from Exeris surfaces *to* agents; the execution layer runs the other way. The layer may consume the bridge's tools — its `docs-guardrails` oracle reads the ADR registry through `docs-list_adrs` and `docs-get_adr` — and that consumption adds nothing to the bridge.

A pull request that adds **capture**, a **dataset** or an **oracle** to the bridge is out of bounds under §A.3, whatever its size. That covers recording agent sessions or tool calls, persisting run records or judgements, and judging whether an agent's work passed — each belongs to `exeris-ai-execution` (§A.1). A reviewer who finds any of them in a diff here is reading an ADR-086 violation, not a feature.

The context-adapter role itself is deferred: it arrives under ADR-025, later, by its own amendment (ADR-086 §"What is NOT in scope"). Until that amendment exists, the bridge exposes no tool aimed at the execution layer.

## When to consult ADR-086

- Any proposed tool, resource or transport whose purpose is to feed, record for, or answer to `exeris-ai-execution`.
- Any change that would make the bridge store what passes through it, rather than serve it.
- Drafting the ADR-025 amendment that defines the context-adapter role.
