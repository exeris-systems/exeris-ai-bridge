# ADR-086 — the AI execution layer, bounded to observation before routing (link stub)

**Authoritative copy:** [`exeris-docs/adr/ADR-086-bound-the-ai-execution-layer-to-observation-before-routing.md`](https://github.com/exeris-systems/exeris-docs/blob/main/adr/ADR-086-bound-the-ai-execution-layer-to-observation-before-routing.md)

ADR-086 fixes what the AI execution layer may do before it may decide: V0 observes and does not route
(§B.5), the seam runs from `exeris-agents` to that layer and never the other way (§A.2), a run record
carries metadata and never the material it was derived from (§C.12), and paired runs — not ambient
rows — are what can support a routing claim (§E.22).

## Why this stub exists

ADR-086 §A.3 binds this repository to one role: **a context adapter to the execution layer, later,
under ADR-025 — never its host.** The bridge's mission runs from Exeris surfaces *to* agents; the
execution layer runs the other way. So nothing here captures runs, holds a dataset, or implements an
oracle, and a pull request that adds any of those to the bridge is out of bounds under the ADR.

The adapter role itself does not exist yet. It arrives by its own ADR-025 amendment, not by extending
a tool family unilaterally, and it stays read-only like every other surface of the bridge (see
[`CLAUDE.md` §"Hard constraints"](../../CLAUDE.md)).

Engineering Protocol 6 of ADR-086 owes this stub; it is paid here.
